How to read these plots

Water years run October 1–September 30; cool seasons October 1–March 31; monsoons June 15–September 30. Cross-year seasons are named for their ending year. The latest started season is shown; after it ends, its completed totals remain until the next season starts.

Daily QC is identical to the existing cumulative plot: trace is zero, missing or flagged reports are not filled, and cumulative lines stop at the first unusable day. A later observed subtotal is only a lower bound. Unsupported comparisons are withheld.

Rank and percentile compare the same start and calendar cutoff in earlier seasons. At least 30 complete matching historical periods are required. Wet rank includes the current season; ties share the best rank. Percentiles use midpoint ties against earlier seasons. Departures and percent of average require at least 20 usable matching periods in 1991–2020; percent of average is withheld for a zero average. These samples can include seasons with gaps after the cutoff.

The cumulative average and middle-50% band use at least 20 complete full seasons from 1991–2020. Historical bounds and closest progression use at least 30 complete earlier seasons, excluding the current season. These may have a smaller sample than the matching-period statistics. Closest progression minimizes cumulative RMSE through the cutoff, breaks ties with the latest year, and is not a forecast.

Historical bars show completed full-season totals. The separate hatched latest-season bar is labeled complete or season to date; a partial bar is never ranked against completed totals. Gaps are unavailable, not zero. February 29 is omitted as a separate plotted point; its precipitation enters March 1 and later totals. On February 29, matching comparisons explicitly stop at February 28 for all years.
