How to read these plots

Missing and flagged observations remain gaps. Trace precipitation counts as zero measured inches and is labeled separately. An incomplete precipitation result is an observed subtotal, not a complete period total. Temperature means use days with both eligible high and low observations; coverage is shown explicitly.

Long selections group daily observations into at most 600 intervals for display. Precipitation bars show observed interval totals; outlined bars are incomplete. Temperature lines show interval averages over paired days. Statistics and CSV exports always use the original daily observations. Extremes here describe the selected period, not historical records. Custom-period historical ranks, normals and anomalies are not calculated in this pilot.

Station and ThreadEx histories may include gaps, moves or component changes; these plots do not establish a homogeneous climate-trend series. Data-through dates reflect the local cache, not a fresh data request.

Performance review

Four concurrent annual-file requests; a 24-year in-memory file cache; at most 600 plotted intervals. Historical files are never loaded for unselected stations. Byte counts describe uncompressed annual JSON payloads; the browser HTTP cache may respond.
