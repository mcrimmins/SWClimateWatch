Reading this preview

Precipitation comparisons need every day. When only the most recent one or two reports are missing (one for a 7-day period), ≥ marks the observed total as a provisional lower bound; no departure or rank is shown until the period is complete. Mean temperature can use at least 95% valid paired high/low days if no gap exceeds two consecutive days. At 98% or more it displays normally; at 95–<98%, * marks the partial current result, including its departure and rank. Dashed edges identify these and other cautions. Missing days are never filled with zero.

The 6- and 12-month options are rolling calendar periods ending on the displayed date; their day counts vary with the calendar. Departures compare the current period with at least 15 eligible corresponding periods from 1991–2020 (15–19 receive a caution). Percentiles and ranks compare one corresponding period per earlier year across the station’s usable record; at least 25 years are required (25–29 receive a caution). Historical precipitation periods must be complete; historical temperature periods use the same 95% rule. A higher percentile means a wetter or warmer period. Ties use a midpoint percentile.

The extremes tab shows the wettest day, wettest three consecutive days, highest high, lowest low, unusually hot days, unusually warm nights, and longest hot and dry spells within the selected period. Temperature thresholds are each station's centered-five-day 1991–2020 90th percentiles; a dry day has less than 0.04 inch of precipitation. Freeze nights (minimum temperature at or below 32°F) use the latest October–March cool season instead of the selected period. The freeze departure is the observed count minus the average count for matching season-to-date periods in at least 15 complete 1991–2020 seasons. Positive means more freeze nights than usual. All these metrics require complete, unflagged observations; missing values are not imputed. They are rolling-period indicators, not historical records or formal annual Climdex indices.

Source: RCC-ACIS station observations. Map boundaries are geographic context only. No live station request is made when this page opens.
