Weather maps

NOAA CPC CORe reanalysis · UTC daily means · Pacific–North America context. The selected calendar date is used exactly; PRISM reporting periods may differ. These maps provide circulation context, not forecasts or evidence of causation.

500-mb contours show absolute heights in dam, with a shared 480–612 dam scale; blue/orange shading is not an anomaly. Purple outlines locate AZ/NM. Jet shading is the speed of daily-mean 250-mb wind components in knots, not a daily maximum or gust. Streamlines show mean flow, not storm tracks. No anomaly baseline is included.

Moisture shading shows total precipitable water (0–3+ inches), with sea-level pressure contours every 4 hPa. Arrows show 850-mb flow, not surface winds or integrated vapor transport. Winds are omitted when hourly terrain-screening inputs are incomplete; the map notes this limitation. H/L labels use daily surface pressure for separate terrain screening and describe filtered daily-mean pressure centers, not tracked storms.

        How to read this explorer

        Source: PRISM Climate Group daily grids via RCC-ACIS. Dates identify the source daily reporting period; they do not imply that all observations cover a local midnight-to-midnight day. Recent grids are preliminary and may be revised. Maps are built from cached PRISM grids after data updates.

        Regional summaries use native grid cells whose centers fall within state boundaries, weighted by cell area. The combined region is weighted by area, not an average of the two state means. At least 95% of the region must have usable values for each displayed statistic. Coverage percentages refer to valid data, not the area affected by an event.

        Daily mean temperature is (maximum + minimum) / 2, using only cells with both inputs. Its normal is the mean of the matching maximum- and minimum-temperature normals. Temperature departures subtract the 1991–2020 calendar-day mean using a centered five-day reference window. Warm/cold coverage for maximum and minimum temperature is the percentage above the 90th or below the 10th calendar-day percentile from that same baseline and window; mean-temperature percentile coverage is not calculated. These are daily coverage measures, not formal annual Climdex indices. Freezing means minimum temperature ≤32°F.

        Precipitation coverage is the percentage receiving at least the stated amount. The spatial 99th percentile describes rainfall across the region that day, including dry cells; it is not a historical percentile. Absolute and seasonal-relative measures are deliberately separate. A zero-rain day can rank in a dry season; it is not automatically noteworthy.

        All dates share fixed map color classes. Maps use native-resolution cells. Missing map cells use the background color, not the zero-value class. Missing reference data can leave departures or percentile coverage unavailable while observed values remain usable. The seven-day comparison is centered on the selected date; the 30-day grid ends on that date. Grids and their timelines can cross season and water-year boundaries using built dates, and retain unavailable days as gaps. Selecting a thumbnail updates the large map and, when needed, the water year and season selectors.
