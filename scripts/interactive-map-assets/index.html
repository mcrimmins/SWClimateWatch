How to read this pilot

PRISM Climate Group via RCC-ACIS. Recent inputs are preliminary and may be revised. Monthly mode uses a fixed October 2026 snapshot; Daily mode uses retained local observations, with dates shown beside the map. This local preview does not update the public site.

Current Conditions shows the latest cached snapshot matching each existing Current Maps product. Precipitation totals support 1/7/30/90 days and water year; percentile ranks support 7/30/90 days and water year; percent of normal supports 30/90 days and water year. Temperature currently offers water-year mean, departure and percentile rank. Period choices follow the metric. Each product keeps its own date; this view has no historical date arrows.

Current percentile ranks use the corresponding 1991–2020 reference, rather than the monthly full-record ranking. They are not record categories. Percent of normal compares amounts with the corresponding average: 100% is average, not percentile 100. Water-year comparisons start October 1 and end on the displayed date. Unsupported comparison cells remain missing, including low-normal precipitation cells; no ratios are reconstructed or filled.

In Current Conditions, choose Variable → Measure → Metric → Period. Mean temperature includes recent 7-, 30- and 90-day rankings and a 30-day departure; high/low temperatures offer 30-day departures. Precipitation change is the change in 30-day percentile over 14 days, in percentile points. Current and longest dry spells pair lengths with percentiles: a dry day has less than 0.04 inch, and higher percentiles indicate longer spells. Ongoing spells show an “As of” date because their start varies by cell. Point comparisons are paired only within the same measure and exact dates.

Temperature extremes count unusually warm days, unusually cold nights and freeze nights over 30 days; freeze anomalies show departures from the normal count. Rainfall character shows 90-day wet-day counts, inches per wet day and the precipitation share from very wet days, paired with percentiles. Wet days have at least 0.04 inch; very wet days exceed the local wet-day 95th percentile. Water-year extremes show the largest complete three-day total since October 1 and its share of water-year rainfall. Shares are not percent of normal. No wet days means intensity is unavailable; shares are withheld below 0.10 inch total.

Colors are drawn from native source cells, without smoothing. Zooming adds geographic context, not climate detail. Clicking returns the source cell, rather than a value inferred from its color. Gray SPI cells have no supported estimate; transparent cells are outside the available data footprint.

Daily mode shows the latest retained local cache, which may lag the public site. Precipitation offers daily totals; temperature offers high, low, mean and departures from the 1991–2020 centered five-day reference. Daily means require paired high/low observations and paired reference cells. Missing cells remain missing. Date arrows move exactly one calendar day; missing dates or unavailable metrics are labeled without substituting another date. Switching variables retains the date; switching modes remembers each mode's period/date and metric.

Select Precipitation or Temperature, then a metric and a 1-, 3-, 6- or 12-month period ending October 2026. Precipitation offers totals, rankings, departures and SPI; Temperature offers means, rankings and departures. Totals are summed; mean temperatures are day-weighted averages. Exact dates follow the selection. Compatible metric choices and the period are retained when switching variables.

Rankings compare matching monthly windows in the available record beginning in 1895. Rank 1 is wettest or warmest; ties share average ranks, which can be fractional. Record categories include ties and require the existing support checks. Common zero precipitation is not labeled record dry. Record codes are never reported as percentiles. Departures compare matching 1991–2020 periods, requiring at least 27 reference years; positive means wetter or warmer. SPI uses a separate fixed 1895–2020 calibration.

Choose Street, Terrain (shaded relief), Satellite (imagery), or None. Basemaps require internet access; climate cells and state boundaries remain available without them. Lower the climate map opacity to see more of the background. Satellite imagery is geographic context, not a current weather or snow observation; image dates vary by location.

Terrain: Esri World Hillshade and source credits. Satellite: Esri World Imagery and source credits. Keyboard users can pan with arrow keys and zoom with +/−; press Enter on the map to query its center.

Terrain and Satellite include optional roads, route labels, boundaries and place names from Esri transportation and place reference services. Detail varies with zoom. These transparent layers sit above the climate colors; turn off Roads & place names for a cleaner climate view. Street already has its own labels. These are hosted Esri services, not Google Maps or an entirely open-source basemap stack.

Leaflet documentation · OpenStreetMap tile usage policy. Only visible basemap tiles are requested; no tile prefetching.
