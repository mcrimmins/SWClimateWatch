Record and interpretation notes

Historical context uses USGS-approved day-of-year statistics from the available record, with at least 30 approved values for a calendar day. Exact reference-year bounds are not supplied by the Statistics API; this is not a fixed 1991–2020 baseline. Metadata record bounds describe observation availability, not the years used in the percentile calculation. Regulation, diversions and watershed changes are not adjusted away.

Daily dates follow USGS station reporting conventions, not the PRISM precipitation window. Categories use published percentile thresholds; exact percentile ranks, historical records and flood categories are not calculated. Tied thresholds (including common zero flows) are identified separately. Seven-day means are not classified against daily thresholds.

Source coverage, workload & how to read this pilot

Source: USGS Water Data API; discharge parameter 00060, daily mean statistic 00003. Provisional observations can be revised. Qualifiers are preserved, and nonnumeric/censored or conflicting values are shown as unavailable rather than zero.

Recent: observation within three days of the cutoff; delayed: four to seven days; stale: more than seven days. These thresholds are local display rules. A recent date with a nonnumeric value still has no usable flow value.

The source inventory includes historical last-observation dates; it is not a count of active or continuously operating gauges. Flood stages, real-time observations and Daily Explorer integration remain separate development steps. This pilot is for local review and is not a warning service.
